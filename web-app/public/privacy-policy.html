Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 1, 2026

RV Learning Hub LMS ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and web platform.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, role (student/faculty/admin), campus, and batch details provided during registration.
  • Academic Data: Course enrollments, video watch progress, quiz scores, attendance records, assignment submissions, and performance analytics.
  • Payment Information: Fee payment records, transaction IDs, and payment method types (Online/UPI/OTC/Cheque). We do not store full credit/debit card numbers.
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, browser type, and app version for compatibility and analytics purposes.
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, features used, session duration, and interaction patterns to improve our services.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain our Learning Management System services.
  • To manage your account, enrollments, and academic progress.
  • To process fee payments and generate receipts.
  • To send academic notifications, announcements, and reminders.
  • To generate performance analytics and leaderboards.
  • To improve our platform, fix bugs, and develop new features.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information with:

  • Institutional administrators and faculty members for academic management purposes.
  • Payment processors for fee transaction processing.
  • Service providers who assist in operating our platform (hosting, analytics).
  • Legal authorities if required by law or to protect our rights.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS), secure password hashing (bcrypt), JWT-based authentication, and regular security audits to protect your data.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. Academic records may be retained for institutional compliance purposes. You may request data deletion by contacting us.

6. Children's Privacy

Our service is designed for educational use by students of all ages under institutional supervision. For users under 13, parental or guardian consent is obtained through the institutional enrollment process.

7. Your Rights

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data.
  • Opt out of non-essential communications.
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the effective date.
